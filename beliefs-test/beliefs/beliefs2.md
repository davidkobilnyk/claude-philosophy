Your beliefs:
1. Abstract objects (numbers, properties) exist independently of minds.
2. Freedom just means acting on your own desires without coercion; determinism is compatible with it.
3. Conscious experience is a further fact that no physical description captures.
4. Some knowledge is available prior to experience, through reason alone.
5. An act is right if it conforms to a duty that could be willed as a universal rule.
6. Moral claims express approval or disapproval, not facts.
7. You persist by being the same continuous biological organism.
8. The state is legitimate only insofar as it protects prior individual rights.
9. The extent of suffering counts decisively against an omnipotent, benevolent God.
10. A good life consists in the exercise of one's distinctive capacities.
