## The Puzzle
"You have two ropes that each take an hour to burn; however, they burn at inconsistent rates. How can you measure 45 minutes? (You can light one or both ropes at one or both ends at the same time.)"

## Solution

**At t=0 minutes:**
- Light Rope A at BOTH ends
- Light Rope B at ONE end

**At t=30 minutes:**
- Rope A completely burns out (lighting both ends → 60 min ÷ 2 = 30 min)
- Rope B is half-burned with 30 minutes of burn time remaining
- Light the OTHER end of Rope B

**At t=45 minutes:**
- Rope B completely burns out
- Rope B's remaining 30 minutes of burn time at double speed: 30 min ÷ 2 = 15 min
- Total elapsed time: 30 + 15 = 45 minutes ✓

## Key Insight
The critical realization is that lighting both ends of a rope makes it burn twice as fast through space but the same amount of material must still burn. Therefore, if a rope takes 60 minutes with one end lit, it takes 30 minutes with both ends lit. This allows us to use rope A as a timer to trigger the lighting of rope B's second end at exactly the 30-minute mark.
