## Game Rules
Question: "You have two ropes that each take an hour to burn; however, they burn at inconsistent rates. How can you measure 45 minutes? (You can light one or both ropes at one or both ends at the same time.)"

Scoring: Accuracy primarily, speed secondarily.

## Solution

The key insight is that burning a rope from both ends doubles the burn rate, making it complete in half the time.

### Step-by-step procedure:

1. **At time 0:00** - Simultaneously:
   - Light Rope 1 at BOTH ends
   - Light Rope 2 at ONE end

2. **At time 30:00** - Rope 1 is completely burned through.
   - Why: Lighting both ends makes the burn fronts meet in the middle, so it takes 30 minutes instead of 60.
   - At this point, Rope 2 has only been burning for 30 minutes from one end, leaving 30 minutes of burn time remaining.

3. **Immediately at time 30:00** - Light Rope 2 at the OTHER end (the previously unlit end).

4. **At time 45:00** - Rope 2 is completely burned.
   - Why: Rope 2 now has 30 minutes of burn remaining. With both ends burning, it takes another 15 minutes to complete.

### Total time: 30 + 15 = 45 minutes ✓

This solution exploits the fact that the burn rate of a rope is irrelevant when we control the burn geometry (lighting from both ends to halve the total burn time). By using Rope 1 as a 30-minute timer, we can then use Rope 2 (with its remaining 30 minutes of capacity) as a 15-minute timer.
